# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sweet-toothed gift buyers and treat shoppers: locals who met the company at fairs and markets, online customers ordering boxes shipped, and office managers ordering corporate gifts. They browse on phones as often as desktops, usually with an occasion in mind (gift, holiday, craving). The site is being redesigned by West Wave Creative for the client (Fabulous Fudge / fabfreshfudge.com).

## Product Purpose

A modern single-page landing site that replaces a dated Square Online template. It must sell the fudge visually (appetite appeal first), make the flavor range easy to browse, let visitors compose a "Build a Box" six-pack of flavors, tell the family story, surface reviews, list upcoming shows/events, and pitch corporate gifting. Success = the page feels hand-made and delicious, and every tab the client asked for (Shop, Our Story, Reviews, Events/Upcoming Shows, Corporate Gifts) is one obvious click away.

## Brand Personality

Buttery, hand-stirred, neighborly. A corner candy shop with a hand-painted sign — copper kettle, marble slab — not a national confection brand. Warm and a little playful in copy ("Reese's ain't got nothing on us"), but the presentation is clean and confident.

## Anti-references

- safudgeco.com, gethsemanifarms.org, fudgekitchens.com — cluttered, dated, clashing brights, too many buttons.
- Anything "too flowery": no script fonts, no swirly ornaments.
- Too much white/blank space — the client wants color and photos doing work, not empty canvas.
- Corporate/high-tech SaaS aesthetics — no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no metric heroes.

## Design Principles

1. **Appetite first.** Real fudge photography leads every major section; the food is the hero, not the chrome.
2. **Color is the shop interior.** Deep-navy drenched sections and the logo's royal blue carry the brand; caramel-gold supplies the warmth next to the fudge photography. White is reserved for product browsing, never as empty filler.
3. **Six things at the top, no more.** Navigation stays exactly the client's tab list; every other action lives in the page flow.
4. **Hand-made, not template-made.** Asymmetry, generous photography, and the client's own playful voice over stock-perfect polish.
5. **The Build-a-Box is the toy.** One genuinely delightful interactive moment (picking six flavors) outweighs scattered micro-effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, full keyboard operability for the flavor picker, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation, descriptive alt text written in the brand voice.
